Security

You choose how the money arrives.

Normal transfer

Straight to the beneficiary's Solana wallet, visible on-chain like any transfer. No fee.

Private (Cloak)

Hidden on Solana. The beneficiary claims it in the Deadman app with a code you send them.

Tiered release

Nothing leaves at once. Each tier has its own deadline and destination, and one check-in stops the clock.

FAQ

Questions nobody likes asking.

What is a dead man's switch?+

A mechanism that acts when you stop responding. In Deadman you check in regularly. If you stop, your release plans kick in.

Who can receive the funds?+

Any Solana wallet, or someone without one through a claim code. The beneficiary's name is only saved on your phone.

What is a release tier?+

A slice of the plan with its own deadline. For example, 25% to Mom after 10 days of silence, then 50% to the kids.

Which tokens are supported?+

SOL and USDC on Solana.

What if I just forget?+

You get nudges when a check-in is close and when it's overdue. Checking in any time before a tier comes due stops the release.
